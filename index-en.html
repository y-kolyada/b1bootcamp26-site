On this page

    Eight sessions, and in each one you give and receive something

    A course about working with AI to produce working products rather than idle conversation. You specify the task, AI executes, the automated check returns Yes or No. Eight times.

    Core philosophy of the course #

    The course teaches how to work with artificial intelligence as an engineer, not as a bewildered chat user. At the heart of every step is a triad: first, your rigorous task specification; second, the machine’s execution; and immediately following, an objective verification free from human subjective opinion. We do not write code for code’s sake, nor do we squander our lifetime on aimless chatter with language models. In every single session, you craft a tangible artifact, release it to the outside world, and permanently upgrade your engineering mindset.

    Three words that define the entire course #

    Task - what needs to be done. Written by you, before asking the AI.
    Action - what actually came out. The text is generated by AI. What belongs in it is your task. What is true in it is your verification.
    Verification - returns GREEN or RED. No opinions, no "almost".

    This site is built by the exact same rules

    It has its own task - zadanie-1.md - written before the AI started working, and its own log - zapis-1.md, showing what it actually did. And potom.md - things we decided not to do right now, with an explicit reason for every line.

    We have no harness that you won’t have. If something is inconvenient for us, it will be inconvenient for you too, and that must be resolved, not hidden.

    How to read a session page #

    Steps are numbered and collapsed: open one, complete it, close it. Next to each step, it states what exists after it - a file, a commit, or a URL. That is the definitive answer to "did it work?".

    There are three paths to the result, and they all arrive at the exact same state:

    • minimum - using the mouse and editor, without the terminal;
    • 100% - using CLI commands, faster and more precise;
    • maximum - involving agents and automated workflows. Will appear later.

    A new session appears here several days before the live session.

    What is required before session 1 #

    The syllabus for all eight sessions - programme.md.

"You possess" means everyone who invested attention into this page: student, reader, parent, or author. And "artifacts" are not merely files and git commits, but new skills, habits, and mental breakthroughs: your own biological neural network has rewired during the work, and that result stays with you forever.
